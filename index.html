PDF Compress

Make scanned and photo-heavy PDFs much smaller by re-saving every page as a JPEG at the resolution you pick. Compare before and after. The file never leaves this device.

Heads-up: every page becomes a picture. Text can no longer be selected or searched, and links, bookmarks and form fields are dropped. Great for scans and photos; for text-only PDFs the result may even be bigger.
